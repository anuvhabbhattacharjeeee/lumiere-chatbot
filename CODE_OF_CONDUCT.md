# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Everyone participating in the Lumière Chatbot project should be treated with respect. We aim to make collaboration welcoming and constructive, regardless of experience, identity, background, or point of view.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Give feedback that is specific, constructive, and focused on the work.
- Assume good faith, listen to differing perspectives, and accept reasonable corrections.
- Keep project discussions relevant and welcoming to contributors of all experience levels.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
